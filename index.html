How we work

A fabricator that asks what the pen is for

MJ KENNELS is a company incorporated under Belgian law, registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0743.680.093, based at Steenovenstraat in 3940 Hechtel-Eksel and trading since February 2020. Its registered seat carries holding company activity and its establishment carries the manufacture of metal structures and parts of structures, which is the work described here: kennels, runs and animal enclosures, and metal structures generally.

We manufacture and nothing else. We do not board animals, breed them, train them or sell them: those require a separate recognition from the regional authority that we neither hold nor claim. What we do take seriously is sizing. Belgian animal welfare rules set minimum enclosure dimensions for establishments that need recognition, and they vary with the size and number of animals. An enclosure below the minimum is unusable in such an establishment regardless of build quality, so we ask about the intended use before quoting, write the internal dimensions onto the quotation, and decline an order specified below the applicable minimum. Separately, load-bearing structural steel is CE marked under the harmonised standard, and we say which items that covers rather than implying it across the range.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Why do you need to know what the kennel is for?

Because it changes the law that applies. Belgian animal welfare rules set minimum enclosure dimensions for keeping dogs in establishments that require recognition, scaled to the size and number of animals. A kennel below that minimum cannot lawfully be used in such an establishment, however well it is built, so quoting without asking would risk selling you something you cannot use.

Can you just build it a bit smaller to fit the space and the budget?

For a private owner's single dog we will discuss what fits, within what is humane. For a licensed breeder, boarding business or shelter, no: if the stated use is an establishment requiring recognition and the dimensions would fall below the applicable minimum, we decline the order. Taking the money and leaving you with an unusable pen is not a saving.

Do you board dogs or have puppies for sale?

No. We are a metal fabricator. Boarding, breeding, sheltering and selling animals all require a separate recognition from the regional authority, which we do not hold and do not claim. If you are looking for boarding or a breeder, we are the wrong company and we would rather say so immediately.

Is everything you make CE marked?

No, and a company claiming otherwise is being loose with the term. Load-bearing structural steel components fall under a harmonised standard requiring certified factory production control, an execution class, welding coordination and a declaration of performance, and those are CE marked. Non-structural items are not covered by that requirement. We tell you which category your item falls into.

Which coating should I choose?

It depends on the exposure, and the honest answer is that cleaning regime matters more than weather. Disinfectants and urine attack coatings that handle rain perfectly well. Tell us what you will wash it down with and how often, and we will specify accordingly — and we will tell you what we cannot warrant if a cheaper coating is chosen against advice.

Do I need planning permission?

Possibly, for a permanent structure, and it is yours to obtain. We will tell you when we think something needs it, and we do not install a permanent structure before it is granted. Fitting first and leaving the permission question with you is how people end up removing something they have just paid for.
